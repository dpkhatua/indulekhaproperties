Who we are

Indurekha Properties Private Limited was incorporated on 10 August 2022 in India. It is registered with the Registrar of Companies, Cuttack, and operates from Ashok Nagar in Bhubaneswar.

The company's registered business is real estate activities with own or leased property. It is governed by a board of directors and reports to the Registrar like any other Indian private company.
